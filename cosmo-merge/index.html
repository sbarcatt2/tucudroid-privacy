Cosmo Merge

Drop planets, merge them into stars and spawn a black hole. Cute & addictive!

Drop, merge, combo! Cosmo Merge is a relaxing yet addictive physics puzzle where you merge cute planets into bigger ones — all the way to a black hole.

  • One-thumb controls, perfect for short sessions
  • Chain merges for combo multipliers up to x3
  • World leaderboard: global, today and challenge boards
  • Daily challenge: everyone gets the same run, 3 attempts per day
  • Share your record as a picture of your board
  • Tiny download, no account needed, works offline
