Privacy Policy for WhatsAPI

Effective date: 7 September 2026
Last updated: 7 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application WhatsAPI (package name com.tdroid.whatsapi, the “App”), published on Google Play by Sebastián Barcatt (“we”, “us”, the “Developer”), handles information.

WhatsAPI lets you open a WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business chat with any phone number without saving that number as a contact first. It builds a standard wa.me link and hands it to the messaging app installed on your device.

We do not operate any server, and we do not have an account system. We do not collect, receive, store or transmit your personal information to ourselves or to any system under our control. Everything you enter into the App stays on your device. The only third party that receives data is Google, through the advertising component described in section 3.

1. Information stored on your device

The App saves the following locally, in Android private app storage (SharedPreferences), so that it can work between sessions. This data never leaves your device, is not readable by other apps, and is not accessible to us.

What is storedWhy
The last country you selectedSo the correct dial code is preselected next time.
Whether you last targeted WhatsApp or WhatsApp BusinessTo reopen the App on the option you use.
Message templates you createSo your saved openers are available again.
Recent chats: the phone number, its dial code, the message text you prefilled, and a timestampTo show the “recent chats” list so you can reopen a chat in one tap.

Deleting this data

  • Delete a single recent entry with the bin icon next to it, or clear the whole list from the recent chats screen.
  • Delete a template with the cross on the template chip.
  • Remove everything at once through Android: Settings → Apps → WhatsAPI → Storage → Clear data.
  • Uninstalling the App deletes all of it permanently.

Because this data is held only on your device, we cannot access it, export it, or delete it on your behalf.

2. Contacts

The App does not request the Android contacts permission and does not read your address book. When you tap “Pick from contacts”, Android's own system contact picker opens. The picker runs outside the App, and it returns only the single phone number you choose. That number is placed in the phone number field. It is not uploaded anywhere, and no other contact is ever seen by the App.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and is funded by a single banner advertisement served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. AdMob is the only third-party service embedded in the App.

To serve and measure advertising, Google may collect and process the following directly from your device. We never see this data in an identifiable form.

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / AD_ID), a resettable identifier used to personalise and limit the frequency of ads.
  • IP address, from which an approximate, coarse location such as country or region can be derived. The App does not request or use precise GPS location.
  • Device and technical information, such as device model, operating system version, language and screen characteristics.
  • Ad interaction data, such as whether an ad was shown, viewed or clicked, for reporting and fraud prevention.

This processing is carried out by Google as an independent party under its own terms. For details, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Your choices about advertising

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: Settings → Google → Ads on your Android device. There you can reset the identifier or delete it entirely, which stops personalised advertising.
  • Opt out of personalised ads: the same Android settings screen offers an opt-out. Ads will still appear, but they will not be personalised.
  • Google's own ad settings: you can review and adjust how Google personalises advertising across its services at My Ad Center.

The App requests no runtime permissions. It does not access contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files, SMS, call logs or the phone state.

5. What the App does not do

  • It cannot read, receive or access your WhatsApp messages or conversations.
  • It has no connection to WhatsApp's systems beyond opening a public wa.me link.
  • It does not send messages by itself. WhatsApp opens with the chat and the text prefilled, and you press send.
  • It contains no analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDK.
  • It does not sell, rent or share personal information with data brokers.
  • QR codes are generated entirely on your device. No number is sent to any service to create them.

6. Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13, or under the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App displays advertising and collects no data itself, it is not designed for, and should not be used by, children without the supervision of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us at the address in section 10 and we will act on it.

7. Data retention

We retain nothing, because we receive nothing. Data stored locally by the App is kept on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App, as described in section 1. Data processed by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's own retention policies, which are set out in the Google Privacy Policy linked above.

8. Security

Information saved by the App is written to Android's private per-application storage, which the operating system isolates from other applications. No credentials or payment information are ever requested or handled by the App. As no personal data is transmitted to us, there is no server-side store of your information to be breached.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, or to object to it, including under the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation and the California Consumer Privacy Act.

Since we hold no personal data about you, requests for access or deletion of data held by us have no data to act upon. You can exercise control over data held locally by clearing it in the App or uninstalling it, and over advertising data through the Google and Android settings described in section 3. You may still contact us with any question or complaint, and you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
