Cosmo Jet

Pilot an astronaut with two turbines. Dodge lasers and climb to the stars!

How high can you go? Cosmo Jet is a fast space arcade game: pilot a little astronaut with a twin-turbine jetpack, dodge lasers and asteroids, and climb from the Moon to the edge of the galaxy.

  • Simple two-thumb controls: easy to learn, hard to master
  • Six zones: Moon, Orbit, Asteroid Belt, Nebula, Galaxy and Beyond
  • Shields, PWR mode and battery cells to power your climb
  • Beat your record and unlock new colors
  • Short runs, no account needed, plays offline
